Inside a working chloroplast

Light splits water on the thylakoids. The ATP and NADPH it makes drive the Calvin cycle in the stroma, where Rubisco fixes CO₂.

Each CO₂ fixed costs 3 ATP + 2 NADPH. Three turns export 1 G3P; the other five G3P regenerate three RuBP. Two G3P build one glucose.

The chemistry

Light (thylakoid): 2H₂O → O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻
Calvin (stroma): 3CO₂ + 3RuBP → 6 3-PGA → 6 G3P
Overall: 6CO₂ + 12H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ + 6H₂O

These plants are called C3 because Rubisco's first stable product, 3-phosphoglycerate, has three carbons.
